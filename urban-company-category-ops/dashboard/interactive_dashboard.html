Execution blueprint

Train on 10 days
Shortlist 12 partners
Coach 57 partners
Test 18 days

1. Detect high shortage-pressure category–zone combinations.
2. Screen & train candidates; activate only verified eligible professionals.
3. Pool adjacent zones where local service capacity fails.
4. Track complaints, punctuality, cancellations and partner allocation.
5. Use a monitored pilot before adopting shift rebalancing.

Peak-shift experiment preserves two slots per partner but did not improve the primary customer performance trade-off. Keep it out of the recommended rollout pending validation.
